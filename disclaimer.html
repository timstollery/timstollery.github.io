About this account and privacy

Information about my account and this website.

A personal account

I wrote much of this account while I was undergoing treatment for testicular cancer, beginning in December 2004. It reflects what I knew, felt and understood at the time. Some treatments, medical guidance and websites have changed since then.

The material is for information and personal reflection. It should not replace advice from a doctor or suitably qualified healthcare practitioner. If you have concerns about your health, seek appropriate professional advice.

Photographs, scans and captions

The photographs and medical images are from my own experience. Some show treatment, surgical wounds or other aspects of hospital care.

Translations

I wrote the diary in English. The translated versions of this website were prepared with the assistance of GPT. Translations may contain errors, particularly in medical terminology. The English text remains the original account.

Privacy and reading preferences

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to help me understand visits and page performance. Cloudflare describes it as a privacy-first service that does not track people across websites. Reading preferences are saved separately on your device so your choices can be remembered on your next visit.

The provider hosting this site may record technical information, including IP addresses, for security and operation. Its privacy policy explains how that information is handled.GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of information.
