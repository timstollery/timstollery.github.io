Reflections

Thoughts along the way

Things I wanted to say that did not fit neatly into the diary.

Personal diaryAbout 4 min read

This is where I post anything that I feel I need to say that doesn't fit within the diary, some of it nonsense, some of it helpful...

Over the past year and half (mid 2003 - 2005) I've dealt with a great deal of personal upset and some stressful times (none of which I will go in to as that is not what this site is about, those of you that know me well, will know enough), so all in all I've had a pretty shitty couple of years just pass by, which in some instances doctors suggest can bring on poor health especially if it's lying dormant there in the first place ready to attack. It might be tough at times, but we must all remember to take stock of life and try not to get too stressed or upset about situations that cannot be avoided or are inevitable. Easy to say, much harder to do. Time is the greatest healer of all. I hope we can all look forward to a brilliant New Year in 2005 with many more to follow.

I'm not ashamed, embarrassed or afraid of my Cancer, this web-site is for all to see from whatever walk of life, gain strength from and be aware for themselves... pass the word around.

Each and everyone of us is made different, so each and everyone of us has a different reaction to everything. Chemotherapy reacts with everyone differently, my experience is here for all to see, if you face it I hope you can stay positive about fighting your condition and not worry too much about the possible side effects from any treatment or surgery. I really hope this site will not only provide a link for all my friends to keep up to date with what's going on with me but also to provide inspiration, positivity and drive to others who need that extra kick. Just go for what you want.

Males... and believe me I speak from experience! If YOU think you have an irregularity down below (yes YOU and YOUR BALLS) or are unsure EVEN AFTER getting yourself checked out, insist on having an Ultrasound Scan, in retrospect if I was offered one I would have known sooner and my Cancer spread would not have been so bad, thus increasing my percentage of making a full recovery from 80 percent to 99 percent!! AND...

KEEP CHECKING YOURSELF AT LEAST ONCE EVERY OTHER MONTH.

General advice for all of you... If YOU are unsure about a health issue EVEN AFTER getting yourself checked out, insist on having it checked again, do some research on the internet. DON'T LEAVE IT, IT MAY NOT GO AWAY.

Mortgages... Critical Illness Cover, do you have it, it might be worth your while thinking about it!

Medical Insurance... if you have it then good on you, but do you know how much it will ACTUALLY cover you in the event that something goes wrong? I pay a fair amount each month and will only get £50 a night for my hospital stays (up to a max of £2000) and NO private care or attention at all, so long as the NHS can treat me within 6 weeks, which in this instance is guaranteed as my life is on the line.

Quite a few people have suggested that I read the Lance Armstrong story or Bob Champions story, they have both suffered from Testicular Cancer and recovered. Of course it's a comfort to hear that people survive and an inspiration that they live on to achieve many wonderful things, but from my discussions with other patients who are fellow sufferers they would rather experience things in their own way not through the eyes and thoughts of someone else. Perhaps after this whole experience has ended I will invest in a copy and be able to relate to their stories, but right now and for example I do not want to be reading about a particular side effect that I just don't want to hear about or will simply not touch me.

Merry Christmas 2004, if Santa brings me anything for this coming year lets hope its my good health and full recovery from this Cancer. I hope he's brought you just what you wished for. Boredom strikes easily at Christmas in hospital, so along with Milton's superb musical talents, Milton, Ken & I created a silly Christmas music video. The recording is no longer available to watch.

My friend Roger thinks a hot curry will cure anything, any one fancy a Chicken Tarka Masala? If only it was that simple, I'd be straight down the nearest ruby house for some Kormatherapy (sorry no link to this one!)

Milton wondered if Cave children ate chicken shaped dinosaurs, do you think the drugs are effecting him?

I hated needles during my treatment, believe me, I was fed up of having them stuck in me... now I'm used to them and could probably do it myself!

This is my personal experience, not medical advice.
