Accessibility and reading

Ways to read, navigate and adjust this website.

Reading this website

The diary is divided into months. Use the contents page to choose a chapter, or the previous and next links at the bottom of each chapter to keep reading. The dates menu links directly to entries within a chapter.

Other languages

Choose a language from the language menu. Translated versions include the complete diary, photographs, scan descriptions and reading controls.

Reading options

The reading-options button in the header lets you choose a larger text size, a light or dark colour scheme, high contrast, or a different reading font. These choices are remembered in this browser. You can also use your browser's zoom controls.

Keyboard and screen readers

If you use a keyboard, press Tab to move between links and controls. The skip link takes you straight to the main content. Press Escape to close an open photograph or settings window. Photographs include text descriptions.

Photographs and films

Select a photograph to enlarge it, then use the previous and next buttons or the left and right arrow keys. The scan animations have written descriptions. The television clip has no captions and the magazine PDF is a scan of the printed page.

Report a problem

Please contact me if you find something difficult to read or use. It helps to include the page, your device and the assistive technology you use.
