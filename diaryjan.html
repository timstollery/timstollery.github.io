The diary

January 2005

Treatment, hospital days and small victories

Personal diaryAbout 43 min read

1st January 2005 - Saturday


Good Morning and Merry New Year, 2005 is upon us and time waits for no one!

After managing to fall asleep sitting upright on the sofa last night, I managed to crawl to bed totally exhausted at about 05.30, still fell asleep with the lights on etc

I still feel fine today, although possibly a little run down as my throat hurts like I've caught an infection, nothing major, it should be gone by tonight. My lungs coped extremely well with the smoky atmosphere last night, although I certainly noticed and felt it a lot more than usual, it'll be the last time I do a long stint out like that for a while.

Going to laze around all day today.

A friend came over and I went shopping in Richmond, picked some hats out from Next in preparation for the hair loss side effect (so far it isn't happening) and browsed around a few sales. Back home now, got a mild headache but it's not stopping me doing anything, probably to do with the sleep depravation of last night.

Cold and horrible outside so I'm not moving from home tonight.

Just watched the 'Manchurian Candidate' on DVD, is this all a conspiracy? Do I want it to be or shall I just be content that this is reality! Well perhaps the powers that be could turn me in to a super hero or something!

Hopefully a full nights sleep tonight.

2nd January 2005 - Sunday


All in all not a bad nights sleep and I'm not sure I had the usual sweats, I might have slept right through them and in them!! Still got this sore throat, more like swollen tonsils, awkward to swallow, but not that bad. I think it's a sign that my body is running lowered immune levels due to the chemotherapy I've had so far, a fact explained more clearly here, it's all about neutrophils!

Very lazy morning, just about to eat my breakfast at 1pm!!

Late lunch and vegetated in front of the TV and internet all afternoon.

Throat still the same tonight, although feels like it might be on its way out.

Ordered Chinese tonight, good for me? I'll certainly enjoy it!!!

3rd January 2005 - Monday


I was very late to bed last night, old/bad habits starting to creep back in there. Scratch the sweating comments of previous, I definitely sweated last night and slept right through it, which I must admit I prefer, instead of waking up two or three times during the night!

It's 1pm again and I'm just about to have breakfast, as I have not long woken up. There's no one around looking out for me at the moment (aahhhh they sigh), so I guess I've slipped back in to my old routines.

I must take on some more water today, I haven't been doing this as well as I had hoped to, whilst out of hospital.

My throat still feels uncomfortable when I swallow. People are starting to comment on my cough and how much noticeably better it has become, almost non existent!

Treatment day tomorrow, but this time I am not as anxious as before.

I've been pulling at my hair for days now with only the odd strand coming out, as normal as can be expected. Today marks the day when quite a few more are starting to come out, certainly a little more than normal, how long before I look like Captain John Luke Picard of the Enterprise?

Finally motivated myself to do the washing up (my Dishwasher broke a couple of days ago). Now I'm showered and off to see my Mum for a bit. I'll be looking forward to coming home for dinner later although I have absolutely no idea what to make myself.

Ok, I decided after a very late lunch indeed, I'm going to have a massive fruit and ice cream blow-out this evening and loads of water to follow. Got to get to bed as early as I can as I'm being picked up in the morning for hospital. Best for me not to drive as I may not be up to getting myself back home in the evening (I was rough last week).

Didn't get my early night after all, oh well I'll manage somehow, it's 2am now I'd better go get my head down. Night...

4th January 2005 - Tuesday


Not a bad sleep, although short. Had breakfast and my tablets, now waiting for my lift to arrive for the hospital. Outpatient treatment day today, hopefully I will feel better than last Wednesday when I return home later. Throat still sore, will check with Docs today, I think I have a small ulcer at the back of my mouth (typical chemotherapy symptom).

Sitting here getting chemotherapy now.

Breaking news... Doc says my neutrophil levels are right down today (0.4 or 400!!) which is not good news at all, but to be expected whilst on chemotherapy, I now have to be really really careful of infection from the outside world, things you would normally breeze past like supermarkets, busy places, children are now all dangers to me. This also now means that I will have to undergo more needles in the form of 3 G-CSF injections over the next 3 days. My neutrophil levels were at 2.4 or 2400 last Wednesday when I arrived, still not brilliant but much better than today.

Well as you all can see I knew I was feeling run down in general as I kept complaining about my throat, I do have a small ulcer on my uvula (the dangly bit at the back of your mouth). Tonight might be a tough one for me, I'll be discharged (I think) but I'll be feeling rough if last week was anything to go by.

So far chemotherapy going well today. More breaking news... I've just received an update on my blood results/history and along with the slightly bad news above I have some good, my HCG tumour markers have now dropped from the original 3900 to 3300 to 805 and now 722!! Soon I will no longer be pregnant!! Despite this I am going to have to take some Antibiotics (Ciprofloxacin 250mg) over the next few days along with the G-CSF injections, to protect and fight any infection that may be lurking.

As Winston Churchill once said amidst one of his famous speeches... "whatever the cost may be, we shall fight on the beaches, we shall fight on the landing grounds, we shall fight in the fields and in the streets, we shall fight in the hills; we shall never surrender" and that's exactly my attitude, mentally and physically!

Home now and feeling better than I did last week, although my lungs feel a little heavy and I'm very, very slightly short of breath if I talk too much, nothing that is concerning me too much. Throat mildly better although it seems to dull in the evening, I now have some Difflan Oral Rinse to alleviate the symptoms if they get worse.

I feel hungry too which is a good thing. A friend has come over to cook me a lovely dinner which I'm looking forward to. The night is young and my lack of sleep last night will serve as a good retirement time tonight.

Managed a little dinner and started my Antibiotics this evening.

Still feeling much the same although in need of the sleep so I've hit the sack at 23.30. Night all...

5th January 2005 - Wednesday


A nice long sleep, I woke early to say good-bye to my friend that stayed over, then went back in to a deep sleep until 11.30am. It's made me a little late for getting back to the hospital today, I should really be there 24 hours after the chemotherapy started yesterday for my G-CSF injection, well I hope to be there by 1pm. I feel fine today, the throat is still there and a little bit more of a cough is apparent. No more for now I must dash, Charing Cross, here I come!

G-CSF Injection went fine and I've spent ages with Milton chatting and helping him out with his Mac this afternoon.

Still feeling fine and will be heading home soon to beat the rush hour.

The hair is really starting to come out now from everywhere! Instead of just a few strands I get a whole bunch if I pull at the hair on my head. Another little symptom I have noticed is that some of the ends of my fingers feel like they've been bruised, it's either a symptom or I've been typing too much on here to you lot!!!

Ate loads for dinner, still keeping up with the tablet schedule (Antibiotics (Ciprofloxacin) and Anti-sickness (Domperidome))

Feeling good this evening, I even briefly popped out to a meeting that I would normally go to on a Wednesday night with the Masons and they've changed the smoking rules in the bar area so it was a nice clear atmosphere to have a chat in. They were very surprised to see me, I wanted them to see that I was looking good, feeling fine and doing well since they heard the shock news almost a month ago now (not many of them have internet access).

Trying to get my head down early tonight as I would like to be on time for hospital tomorrow for my injection at 12pm. Then, all being well I'm going to take a drive out to my friends shop for a healthy lunch and to spend some time there.

6th January 2005 - Thursday


Slept well, although woke with a very mild headache (I stayed up a bit too late I think), it's fading away now as I eat my breakfast. Got to get ready for my 'day out' hospital first then Maidenhead. I'll be on the net so keeping you all up to date.

Hospital visit was a breeze, injection went well, these ones are easy! Briefly caught up with Milton, he's doing fine but still isolated and confined to his room.

Got to Maidenhead a bit later than expected, the M4 motorway was closed due to a major accident and I got stuck in the subsequent jam!!

Had a nice lunch from M&S and now chilling out in my friends shop in Maidenhead (I'm actually configuring their shiny new 20" iMac).

OK a bit of a health update... symptoms I have had so far or currently suffering from...

Hair loss (ongoing and slow, recent)
Feeling of bruised finger tips (very mild but ongoing, recent)
Sicky feeling in back of throat (very very mild, early symptom)
Mouth Ulcers (one, disappearing, recent)
Sore Gums (very very mild, recent)
Temperature and chills (occasionally)
Constipation (major & very very painful to go!)
Cramps (one off so far, in feet & one leg)
Sweating (ongoing during sleep)
Cough (mild, occasionally)

It's not all that bad, I can cope!

Got in, had dinner, had a long hot salt bath (I'll leave it you to work out what I'm trying to heal, it's round the back!)

Now sleep......

7th January 2005 - Friday


Slept very well although I stayed up a bit too late again last night (02.30), my hospital stay next week will get me back in to a good routine. Noticed the sweating a lot last night, it didn't wake me but seemed that when I did wake up, everything was a lot wetter and colder than normal (yuk!) and NO I didn't wet myself, that's not a symptom, thank you!!!

Getting ready for the last hospital injection today and then freedom for the weekend before Phase 2 of my major chemotherapy starts on Monday. There'll be lots to report next week!

Back from the hospital, injection went fine, nurse was in a little bit of a hurry today so I felt it a little more than normal but it went just fine for me. Both my sisters with me today and one of them didn't feel too well at the thought of my injection, so I had to sit her down after she nearly blacked out!

Bumped in to my Dr. today, he had some news for me. My HCG tumour marker levels as of Tuesday the 4th Jan 2005 are now down to just 75!! He commented that this is a very impressive result considering we are only at the early stages of my treatment. I do not know my current neutrophil levels but these should be back up to normal very soon as my current course of G-CSF injections have now finished.

Is this a sign of positive thinking plus the right course of medication? I think so! Lets keep our fingers crossed that I can maintain my 'impressive' battle levels!

Feeling quite tired tonight but I must cook myself some dinner otherwise I'll be feeling even worse in the morning. Getting someone to do my shopping for me is quite embarrassing as I certainly feel like I should be doing it myself, only thing is the less exposure to the general public in the outside world the better (increased risk of infection being the only reason). So a good friend is coming over in the morning to collect my shopping list and pay a much needed visit to Sainsbury's.

Hair really starting to come out now, just so much as touching my head will produce a flurry of hair, all over this keyboard I'm typing on! Do I let it fall or shave it? I'm sure a shower in the morning will result in a blocked plug-hole at this rate!

Midnight now and I'm going to try and get my head down after a quick bath.

Night all...

8th January 2005 - Saturday


Slept really well, nice knowing that I don't have to be up and ready for anything in particular today. I might go and confine myself to my friends shop in Maidenhead (I stay out of the way of customers etc as I can relax in one of their many demonstration rooms) just for a change of scenery, apart from that nothing major planned at all.

Remember me telling you about blocking the plug-hole in the bathroom, well it happened today in the shower. I attempted to wash my hair and instead of transferring shampoo to my head, my head transferred hair to my hands! A surreal experience, but it's all part of the healing process. I now have a significantly lighter head of hair on my shoulders, it's going to be time to do the 'shave it all off' soon!!

Went to my Mum's house on the way back from Maidenhead to show her how thin my hair looked and to reiterate to her that it was all part of my recovery and not to worry about it. Once home to Hampton I made myself a quick something to eat and now sitting chilling out in front of the box.

Finally got to bed at 02.30, another late night!

9th January 2005 - Sunday


Again despite going to sleep quite late, I slept really well. Not wanting to tempt fate, over the past 2 nights I really haven't noticed any night sweats!? Have they gone I wonder? I'll keep you updated.

I had a weird dream that I had gone to sleep and when I had woken up someone had Cannulated my bad hand (I must have fallen asleep on it and one of the final things I did last night was to remind myself that I must take my Emla cream back to hospital with me). When I say my bad hand, my left hand still hurts after a central vein was burnt or irritated by my chemotherapy drug Etoposide being left stagnant for too long without being flushed after a drip treatment. This isn't a side effect, this is due to lack of hospital staff and me not realising the urgency of its need to be changed (I haven't made that mistake again since). I am constantly applying Arnica cream to the back of my hand to hopefully aid in the damaged veins repair.

Today marks the 1 month anniversary of my visit to A&E and the early stages of my Cancer diagnosis by the worried doctors at West Mid, back on the 9th Dec 2004. Not really a celebration, but I've certainly come on a long way in a short time so far, in recovery, medical knowledge and mortality of life teachings!

Another health update for you, I'll try to keep these up regularly now. Symptoms I have had so far or currently suffering from...

Hair loss (ongoing, now rapid, recent)
Feeling of bruised finger tips (diminished, recent)
Sicky feeling in back of throat (gone, early symptom)
Mouth Ulcers (gone, recent)
Sore Gums (very very mild, recent)
Temperature and chills (after 6 hour Bleomycin treatment only)
Constipation (major & very very painful to go, always. I am already using a cream and making changes to diet to alleviate this which has been most definitely brought on by the anti chemo sickness drugs I have to take, a small price to pay in the grand scheme of things)
Cramps (one off so far, in feet & one leg, very mild feeling of an attack coming on in same areas but nothing since)
Sweating (during sleep, nothing in past 2 nights)
Cough (mild, occasionally)

Hopefully on Monday I will get a chance to have a longer sit down with my Consultant as he promised a detailed output and explanation of the history of my blood results, which I can then relay to you here. I also have a few more questions to ask that have arisen over the past couple of weeks.

I should be having an X-ray on Monday, this will occur at the beginning of each Phase of my treatment, hopefully these images will soon be posted up on this site for all to examine! I'm also working with the chap that runs one of the most advanced imaging departments in the UK at West Mid hospital to bring my CT scans to life here on the site!!

Been busy today creating a message board for all of you to register at and post messages.

Late to bed again! 02.00 and I'm off to bed, hospital soon, end of my freedom for a bit and more importantly embarking on Phase 2 of my chemotherapy.

10th January 2005 - Monday


I'm up a little bit late and getting a few final bits ready to leave for my week in hospital. I have shed so much hair in the past couple of days both in the day and especially on the pillow at night that I will have no choice but to shave my head today, as it is looking very thin in places, a proper case of Alopecia!

Arrived at the hospital, bed was ready but it looks like I'm going to have to wait a while before they get organised here to take my blood, take an X-ray and start my chemotherapy. The first round is 15 hours long so I'm sure it will go well in to the night tonight, guess who's not going to get much sleep this week!

15.40 now, blood test complete, X-ray complete. Blood test results are back with a good enough neutrophil level to begin chemotherapy (when that is, your guess is as good as mine, currently we are looking at this first stage finishing at 07.00 tomorrow and that's only if they start at 16.00!).

My Consultant came round to have a chat with me a short while ago. He has now seen my latest chest X-ray and is very impressed with the result, I am responding to the treatment very well and so far so good. Along with my impressively low HCG tumour marker results from the other day, it's all looking very positive indeed. A big boost for my already positive and strong attitude towards getting through this. I have also confirmed that my Testicular Cancer is of a nonseminoma type, although not having had a biopsy or Orchiectomy, due to my urgent need for chemotherapy, I will not know the specific type for sometime. This isn't a good or bad development, just stating facts.

I did manage to get a look at my latest X-ray at the time of it being taken earlier as I was having an interesting chat with the radiographer. I must say, although with an untrained eye, they looked substantially different to the previous X-ray I had seen. Soon I hope to have them up here on my site for you all to see the great progress I am making so far for yourselves!

Started my 15 hour chemotherapy at 18.00 (roll on 09.00 tomorrow when I can have a bit of freedom from the IV machine). The cannulation wasn't too much of a pleasant experience today, where I had my arm numbed with the Emla cream the vein wouldn't play ball and was difficult to get to (the nurse stuck the needle right through it, "it's blown" she said), so I had to opt for another place which hadn't been anaesthetised, at least it went in first time and hopefully will last for the next 5 days.

Ken is back from his well deserved break away for the New Year.

Shaved all my hair off tonight (with a little help from Ken), doesn't look that bad at all. The first time in my entire life I have had a shaved head. I expect I will wake up feeling weird in the morning with a cold head! New experiences all the time!

I'm in bed now at 23.30, chemotherapy still going on. I've had a nightmare to get the only one nurse to change my IV bag, at this rate I will have to set my own alarms to make sure my IV bags are changed throughout the night at the correct times. It's already a battle to beat the cancer & now a battle to be treated on schedule in here too, especially tonight. One trained chemotherapy nurse to look after some 30 patients, most of whom need regular attendance. They don't even give school teachers that amount of responsibility with the number of children they can look after, do they? She looks really stressed too, doesn't make for good judgement!

A long night ahead, I guess at this rate I will be free of the IV at around 10/11 in the morning. Good night.

11th January 2005 - Tuesday


Wow, what a night. The whole of my ward on chemotherapy, machines constantly bleeping as the one nurse on duty could not cope with the attendance required. My final IV bag was changed extremely later than it should have been, a requirement of chemotherapy administration is that two nurses confirm your identity & the drug being administered. One nurse does not magically turn in to two, she had to wait 3 hours for assistance to come from another ward here on the 6th floor.

I wasn't even weighed after one of my IV bag changes and I know that it is a particular requirement for it to be carried out to be sure I am not retaining fluids.

So I'm fighting Cancer, treatment schedules & sleep depravation now! Do they want me to get better?

That's my tired grumpy old man bit out of the way. I hope to be free of my IV machine in some 3 hours from now (09.20) and starting IV again at 15.00 (I wish).

I did get some sleep but it was most certainly frequently broken. Slept with my hat on all night as ward was a little chilly and I'm not used to having no hair! The hats will be a permanent feature for a while now.

I've been very tired and grumpy all day today, not good company by a long shot.

Back on the chemotherapy at a time I'm a bit happier with now (16.00) as I understand and am working with the correct BEP regime's timings and not that of the hospitals politics.

It seems to me that the nurses here are trying to get me out of hospital for Friday, which I just cannot see happening as my daily treatments run well in to the late hours. Bringing my day to day chemotherapy timings closer together will make no real difference to the way in which the drugs work, but I do like to have a bit of time to recover -as advised- between treatments. Therefore I feel it is best to have a minimum 22 to 24 hours between start times and thats just what I intend to have, nothing else!

Well I'm feeling very tired and a bit low so not going to go into too much detail tonight. My chemotherapy will finish in the early hours, and I hope to get some sleep to allow me to have a slightly better day tomorrow.

I've managed to get a snap of my Chest X-ray this evening and they've now promptly appeared on the site, with my HCG results to follow tomorrow, and CT scans hopefully sometime soon. I feel positive after seeing the difference between the two Chest X-rays, especially as they are only a month apart. It certainly justifies taking the treatment and all its side effects on board when you can see clear improvements like these, but I'm not kidding myself as there's still a long way to go.

That's it, I'm off to rest and try for some of those precious Zzzzz's.

Catch up with you all tomorrow.

12th January 2005 - Wednesday


I was left well alone to sleep today and although the ward was noisy from about 08.00 I managed to stay undisturbed until 10.00. So all in all a better nights sleep was had.

Once again today I've been cajoled in to 'complying' with the Hospital system and have agreed to meet in somewhat middle ground on my chemotherapy treatment times. Really the whole point of the matter is that they -being the hospital system- want me out of the bed on Friday and out of the hospital as the West ward closes on Friday and it would be a nightmare for them to allocate me another bed in another ward. I really don't mind staying another day as my recovery is paramount, whereas some patients would want to be at home, I feel better cared for in here than I would be at home, obviously as I am ill, surely the best place for me is in hospital!

So in order for me to be out of here on Friday I am sitting it out until they are ready to put the padlock on the West wards doors, I shall then be ready to leave. Bringing my chemotherapy times closer and closer together each day. My point being that I want and need time to convalesce each day and have some freedom from the drip.

In an ideal world 24 hours between chemotherapy start times would be the best way to be treated (at least from my experience and from what I've been told from my Consultant), although it makes no real difference to the drugs effectiveness, it gives the patient time to both physically and more importantly mentally convalesce between treatments, as we all know this is not an ideal world by far, especially when being processed in the NHS 'system'.

So it'll be a 15.00 start today and a 13.00 - 14.00 start tomorrow then a 10.00 start on Friday, each days treatment lasting for just over 9 hours.

Oh joy, the first chemotherapy drug to be administered is not ready, despite the hospital wanting to get me started as early as possible after I was forced in to agreeing to cut my personal recovery time. I'm here but the drugs aren't?? Apparently they'll be ready at 17.00 now, which means an 02.00 finish and another sleep deprived night! Isn't sleep a good thing when you are trying to recover?

So a bit of advice so far in my journey, if your coming to hospital (which I hope you don't have to) you WILL NOT be treated like a human, you WILL be treated just as a number. I know that it's no one individuals fault, even though at times they take it as such when I question, but what am I meant to think or feel when everyday there are constant changes to my treatment schedule. Apparently and according to the Sister today I would only be kept in beyond Friday for medical reasons. Someone tell me if I'm going crazy here but I thought I had Cancer?

You may read in books and hear from Cancer patients that one of the biggest parts of the battle is your mental attitude towards your recovery. Perhaps they meant the mental battle against the hospital system and not the Cancer? Mine has most certainly been scarred this week after all this back and forth with different stories from different people, all the time.

Certainly feeling a little better this evening compared to how I was feeling last night. I was actually sick once last night and again a little this morning. The sickness is due to the chemotherapy and also induced by my mental state of mind and sleep depravation (thanks hospital system)! The feeling of sickness has passed now and I hope to get a reasonable amount of sleep tonight, although my chemotherapy will not finish until 02.30.

Had quite a few visitors again this evening which was a nice surprise, it's all gone quiet now so I'm off to get my head down for a bit before my next IV bag change is due.

See you tomorrow for another stress filled, sleep deprived day!

13th January 2005 - Thursday


chemotherapy finished at 04.00 this morning so all in all a slightly better but slightly disturbed nights sleep.

Had a nice relaxing Aromatherapy massage this morning. Walking around my ward a bit, I'm being met with muted grins from the Sister and some of the nurses, they obviously don't like the fact that, although somewhat unsuccessfully, I tried to stand my ground with regard to my treatment times. It's still playing on my mind.

Perhaps I'll have one more go today to try and bring them around to my way of thinking but I just don't think it will work!

chemotherapy due to start at 14.00 today.

Had a few visitors today and this evening, didn't feel quite right all day, still very tired. Turned down my dinner again.

Hopefully will get to sleep not much after midnight. Night.

14th January 2005 - Friday


Morning... definitely a better and fuller nights sleep, I really needed that. I managed some Cornflakes this morning which should give me a good start for what appears to be a heavy and rushed day ahead.

Started chemotherapy at 11.00 today and will be discharged around 18.30 and home this evening.

Got home by 19.30 and fell asleep almost immediately on the sofa.

Certainly not feeling 100 percent tonight.
Didn't eat at all.

Retired to bed at 01.00

15th January 2005 - Saturday


Woke up at 09.30 after sleeping right through, still not feeling that good but I've taken my medication and will hopefully get some breakfast soon, once I feel like eating.

It's 11.45 now and I'm still not feeling great or like eating, got to try and force something in otherwise I'll have no energy to get through my first day at home.

Managed a bowl of cereal for breakfast. A few friends paid me a visit early afternoon, which lifted my spirits.

Sticking to the medication regime of 2 Domperidome tablets 3 times a day for anti-sickness.

I've stayed in all day watching TV and doing nothing much at all! A very late lunch of soup and bread, then got a little hungry late this evening and managed a small dinner but nothing like the quantity I am used to having.

Another good nights sleep ahead.

16th January 2005 - Sunday


Slept well last night. Still a feeling of sickness about me but I managed some breakfast and of course my usual medication.

It certainly feels like the fight has been knocked out of me a bit over this past week. What with one thing and another (read last weeks hassle at the hospital), it certainly has left me feeling deflated, a lot different to the way I was feeling after my first full weeks treatment.

Feeling slightly revived after a long bath and a shower!

Off to see my Mum this afternoon, then a friends and maybe something more to eat later.

I managed a wonderful roast dinner this evening and even some cheese on toast when I returned home, normal appetite returning, I hope so.

EDTA test starts in the morning tomorrow, will take about 4 hours to complete.

Better try and get some sleep!

17th January 2005 - Monday


Not a bad nights sleep although it seemed short as I didn't go to bed until late. Awoke with the feeling of sickness but it certainly feels a lot milder now.

After managing some cereal for breakfast at home I left for the Hospital. Arrived for my EDTA test in reasonable time, although a little late.

First injection out of the way now, just killing 2 hours before the first of the blood tests is carried out. After a reasonable lunch and a bit of mulling about, another 2 hours passed and back for the second blood test, a quick visit to the Macmillan/CancerBacup Centre at Charing Cross for a chat and then off home.

Yet another chemotherapy treatment day tomorrow, I also have a visit to the 9th Floor to present myself as a case study and model patient (yeah, if they treated me like one) to some medical students (I had previously agreed to this). It's in the afternoon so not quite sure how hot I'll be feeling after a mornings worth of chemotherapy and I'll still be with my IV machine when in front of them.

Relaxing tonight and another visit to see my Mum.

Not much for dinner tonight, running around too much and nothing really prepared. My Dad is visiting for the rest of the week so he'll make sure I'm well fed.

Night.

18th January 2005 - Tuesday


Got myself some cereal for breakfast and left for the Hospital. chemotherapy day today, procedure slightly different as I was sent to another area 'clinic 8' for my blood test and a quick chat with one of my doctors.

Bloods have to be taken in order to establish my immune system level and whether I can be given treatment, on this occasion I've been told 'we'll treat you whatever the result'.

Now back on familiar territory in outpatient East on the 6th floor waiting for my Emla cream to take effect and be connected for my chemotherapy blast.

Should be able to get some more HCG tumour marker results today to go with the already impressive figures that are now shown in detailed graph form on my results page along with my X-rays.

Cannulation went well today, I'm all connected and running now. Feeling good for treatment, let's see how this lot makes me feel later tonight!

The medical students await, 2pm and I'll be presented in front of them at their meeting on a different floor. I've been disabled a little bit today as the Cannula is connected on my right wrist so I cannot use my laptop properly (Ahhhh!).

The presentation of a model Cancer patient went well in front of the students, Testicular Cancer diagnosis and symptoms were discussed and my case in particular was presented. This went out from the amphitheater at Charing Cross live to about 6 other hospitals, transmitted via a very professional video link setup (a couple of pictures are in my image gallery). Made my way back to the care of 6 East to sit and relax whilst the rest of my chemotherapy finished.

Straight home and ate some dinner that my Dad had prepared for me, I even went for seconds and had some afters! Just chilling out in front of the TV and am feeling relatively OK in comparison with other days that I have finished my outpatient treatments.

19th January 2005 - Wednesday


Slept for about 10 hours, at last a long undisturbed nights sleep. All that faces me today is the first of 3 G-CSF injections which I am getting my friend Ken to do for me to save going back to the hospital.

Tried to get some things done today so was out and about for a good part of the day, although energy levels were quite low at times. Also managed to get some important paperwork dispensed with.

This evening I have eaten well and dozed off on the sofa. Fortunately I had my phone on silent so my relaxing went undisturbed as some people still insist on calling me incessantly.

Thought I'd give you an update on the health/symptoms front...

Hair loss (it's all gone/shaved off)
Feeling of bruised finger tips (comes and goes)
Sicky feeling in back of throat (more apparent during inpatient stay, dies off once out of hospital after 3 days)
Mouth Ulcers (gone)
Sore Gums (very very mild)
Temperature and chills (after 6 hour Bleomycin treatment only, unnoticed recently)
Constipation (major & very very painful to go, always. I am already using a cream and making changes to diet to alleviate this which has been most definitely brought on by the anti chemo sickness drugs I have to take, a small price to pay in the grand scheme of things)
Night Sweats (occasionally, undisturbed by them)
Cough (almost non existent)
Tiger stripes on skin/rough skin (apparently down to the Bleomycin in my system, a chemo side effect)
Tiredness (frequently)

20th January 2005 - Thursday


A good nights sleep, I woke up relatively early too. Going to keep myself busy with some DIY today!

Can't forget I've my second of 3 G-CSF injections to have around 1pm today and tomorrow. I have to get them out of the fridge about 30 minutes before, otherwise it's like injecting liquid nitrogen into my arm!

A little bit of work, DIY and G-CSF injection out of the way. A late lunch now and time to get on with some more DIY (with my Dad of course).

Some running about this evening to sort things out for my Mum and back for a big roast dinner (courtesy of my Dad!). My sister has come to visit, so it's going to be a full house tonight.

21st January 2005 - Friday


Up early today and from a full house it'll soon be empty (was it something I said?), everyone is leaving today, my sisters gone, my Dad soon and Ken later.

Got my final and third G-CSF injection later today, apart from the usual Domperidome anti-sickness tablets, thats all the hospital stuff I need until Tuesday of next week.

Today's injection was a little more uncomfortable than others, but it's out of the way now. These bruised feelings on my finger tips and hands are more apparent too, some mildly swollen.

After some breakfast, I've been asleep on the sofa for most of the morning.

Have you all seen the latest pictures in my Gallery, I've revealed my baldness!! Great news, NOT! It appears I have early signs of Oral Thrush, "how do I know", I hear you say! Well I remember a couple of fellow patients contracting it and no I wasn't snogging them before you ask! 'Candida albicans', we ALL have a little of it in our mouths, for those of us that have healthy immune systems (unlike me at the moment) it's no problem for our bodies to deal with. So until the Doc prescribes me something, I'm going to try the natural yoghurt remedy, must get myself down to the supermarket in a mo.

After a visit to the supermarket (I got carried away and bought loads of stuff), I promptly returned and ate a whole pot of natural yoghurt, then made myself a late dinner. After further investigation I found out that the Corsodyl already prescribed to me will treat Oral Thrush. I will still check with the Doc to see if there isn't now a further requirement for more meds when I next see him.

Got to bed quite late for me, but then I would normally be out a lot later on a Friday night, something I'm beginning to miss a lot.

22nd January 2005 - Saturday


Having a lazy morning today, still in bed and it's nearly 12pm! I think the night sweats have gone now, I seem to sleep right through and cannot say that I have noticed anything, even when I wake up. Going to compile another symptom list for you all today, it changes so often!

I'm 33 and 5 months old today! What an interesting fact.

Must get up and get some breakfast.

Been dosing on and off most of the day today, made myself some sandwiches for lunch and now planning dinner.

Here's that list I promised earlier...

Tiredness (frequently)
Hair loss (it's all gone/shaved off)
Bruised fingers, small sores appearing (strange!)
Oral Thrush (early stages)
Tiger stripes on skin/rough skin (apparently down to the Bleomycin in my system, a chemo side effect)
Bowel Movements (very very painful. I am already using a cream and making changes to diet to alleviate this which has been most definitely brought on by the anti chemo sickness drugs I have to take, a small price to pay in the grand scheme of things)
Night Sweats (gone?)
Cough (almost non existent)
Temperature and chills (after 6 hour Bleomycin treatment only, unnoticed recently)
Sicky feeling in back of throat (more apparent during inpatient stay, dies off once out of hospital after 3 days)

I cooked a big dinner and stuffed myself full of goodness, yogurt and fruit for dessert. Watched the box for most of the evening, now off to bed. Night.

23rd January 2005 - Sunday


Woke up quite early for a Sunday. Got a bit of an upset stomach today, probably something I ate yesterday.

I will make an effort and get out somewhere today, perhaps some shopping or just a drive over to my Mums house.

Scratch the shopping, amazingly I've spent all day asleep, only having just woken up at 17.00! Not sure why I've been so tired. I'm up now and am off to see my Mum.

Spent the evening with Mum and returned home for a late dinner. I hope it settles better in my stomach this time. Going to let it digest and then hit the sack.

24th January 2005 - Monday


Woke up early again, must force myself to stay awake today for an early night tonight. It's hospital day tomorrow and I have to be there by 09.30 to get things started on time.

Stayed awake so far, it's been raining heavy outside, one of those really dark overcast downfalls, so a bit depressing. I'm going to venture out soon, once it brightens. Got to buy some more food and go wander around the local DIY shop for something to do.

This 'burnt' vein on my left hand still bothers me, I must get it looked at. Looking back through my diary it happened back on the 15th December 2004! I will try and get a picture up on here soon so you can see it.

Had a simple but filling three course dinner tonight, still home alone. Getting anxious about my hospital visit tomorrow. Been in touch with Milton this evening and like me he's experiencing a wait for his treatment to begin, a simple course to a heavy schedule a lot like my last week in hospital.

This is where I take a deep breath, close my eyes and hope tomorrow is over and done with quickly. Perhaps someone could arrange it such that I don't wake up tomorrow, receive my needed treatment and wake up on Wednesday morning instead, alas I think not.

Night.

25th January 2005 - Tuesday


Apologies for the lack of updates today. I had to rush out this morning to be at the hospital on time and I've had some trouble updating from the hospital today.

Had a quick breakfast and was off to the hospital. Bloods taken as always before chemotherapy can be started. Once the results came back my neutrophil reading was quite low (0.8) and something that hasn't really been mentioned before, my platelet reading was low (52,000). So not only am I prone to infection, I'm also now low on the materials (associated with blood coagulation) needed to fight an infection. This is to be expected on chemotherapy but has to be monitored very carefully, even simple infections or a nosebleed could be a danger to me. A further blood test is booked for Thursday of this week.

chemotherapy was initiated despite the low readings and the cannulation went well, my trusty Emla cream saw to that.

Had a brief visit from my registrar whom I discussed my ailments with, I've been given a prescription for a different mouthwash. He checked my burnt vein which I have been complaining about and assured me that although it will take time it will eventually repair itself.

Outpatients ward very busy today, a lot of comings and goings. I gave up my seat and moved out in to the reception area with my IV machine to continue treatment, this suited me as there are actually and unbelievably more electrical outlets here than in the outpatient ward!

Shortly after moving my cannulation went wrong! It did something called 'tissue'ing', where the vein leaks the chemotherapy in to the local tissue for any number of reasons. Dangerous with certain chemotherapy drugs, but fortunately for me the Bleomycin isn't to bad an irritant and didn't seem to have much effect on surrounding tissue. 99 percent of the time this means relocating the cannulation, this time without my Emla to protect me, I had to go through the full experience.

Nearly finished now, feeling quite tired and looking forward to getting home.

Paid a visit to some fellow patients before I left the hospital (i felt like a doctor on his rounds). This tired me even more and Ken promptly drove me home.

By this time I was starting to feel the effects of my Bleomycin chemotherapy, exhausted and shivering.

Had a very light dinner of soup and bread, watched the box for a bit and then had a long hot bath. Straight to bed and I was asleep within minutes.

26th January 2005 - Wednesday


Woke up at 09.00 this morning. Feeling rotten, got a headache and feeling drowsy. I've slept a bit more this morning hence the late diary entry. Cant manage much more than this at the moment, I'll get back to you all with a better update later...

Feeling much better now, I really wasn't up to much this morning and well in to the afternoon. Now I'm up, showered and dressed. I've had some breakfast and feel alive again! I'm not going to over do it, will eat well and may pop out this evening.

I mentioned yesterday that I was prescribed another stronger mouthwash for my Oral Thrush, well I was so out of it I hadn't realised this prescription was actually tablets. I still have the usual Corsodyl mouthwash which I am using regularly. I also now have a course of Fluconazole tablets to take, which I will start this evening.

I think I will rattle soon with the amount of medication I am taking, most of this is to overcome side effects of chemotherapy! Here's a quick recap...

Domperidome 2 tablets 3 times a day for the foreseen future, for anti-sickness.
Granisetron given by IV before chemotherapy and taken in tablet form each day of chemotherapy and for 2 days after, for anti-sickness.
Fluconazole 1 tablet daily for 5 days, for Oral Thrush.
Corsodyl mouthwash twice daily, for Oral Thrush.
Preparation H for piles which have been caused by constipation, a side effect of the anti sickness drugs!
Paracetamol when required, for pain, headaches etc

A very nice dinner and I popped out to meet up with the other members of my Masonic Lodge. They were surprised to see me, I'll be seeing most of them again this Saturday at a supper meeting.

A new symptom tonight, pain in my groin on the right side. I wonder if this is associated with my Testicular Cancer, being in a similar area I wouldn't be surprised if it is. Hopefully I'll bump in to the Doctor tomorrow to bring this up.

Feeling OK but tired now, so off to bed for a full and recuperative nights sleep.

27th January 2005 - Thursday


Awake and feeling OK today, going to wait for my Dad to arrive, then I'll set off for my surveillance blood test at Charing Cross.

Relaxed for a bit then Ken drove me to Charing Cross for my blood test. Test went fine and had to wait a while for my results to come back.

Platelet reading at 68,000 now, which is very slightly better but still not quite enough! Neutrophil reading at 0.8 which is still low and the same as it was on Tuesday morning.

Cleared to have two G-CSF injections, one tonight and one tomorrow.

I've also finally managed to get a new HCG reading, which will mean the chart will be updated soon. It's now at a measly 10, very good indeed, however the battle isn't over yet!

Collected the G-CSF injections from the hospital pharmacy and left for home, popped in via Mum's house to drop off some things.

Now I'm home, starving hungry and ready for a big dinner.

Dinner and a stack of prunes with ice cream for afters. Gave myself a bit of digestion time whilst watching the box and headed for a hot bath and bed.

28th January 2005 - Friday


Slept for a good 10 hours, feeling good today. No plans, just relaxing.

New pictures in the image gallery for you to look at and the HCG results chart has been updated.

Big decision made today, I've decided to book flights to Leeds and go to a show in Manchester during my treatment. I have spoken with my Consultant about this and he didn't see any problem in doing it providing it didn't get in the way of my treatment schedule, which if all goes to plan I will be free that weekend.

I'm going to fly up and visit some friends in Leeds, stay with them at their house and they will look after me and accompany me to the show; Cirque Du Soleil's - Saltimbanco.

I spoke to British Midland and they will refund the full cost of my ticket if I have an official Doctors letter regarding my treatment so it wasn't too big a financial gamble and gives me something to really look forward to in the coming month.

I am a big Cirque Du Soleil fan and what better show to attend than Saltimbanco, a celebration of life!

Now I'm quite excited about going to see my friends whom I haven't seen for ages and the show. It could also be a good opportunity for some of my family that live that way, a chance to visit me whilst I'm there for the weekend. If all goes to plan I will arrive the evening of Thursday 10th Feb, see the show on the night of Saturday 12th Feb and return to Heathrow the evening of the 13th Feb.

I have really been chilling out today, I can honestly say I haven't done anything at all apart from drink and eat of course! I must try and motivate a little this evening.

Ate some dinner but didn't really feel to good this evening, a mild stomach ache and tiredness. Watched the box until quite late and retired for the night. I didn't even bother to get dressed today!

29th January 2005 - Saturday


Woke up feeling quite good this morning, well much better than last night.

I've had a bath and a shower, now for a big breakfast.

I have a busy day ahead. After today, only one more day of freedom left, then I become just a patient number, not a free man!

Relaxed for the afternoon, tiredness still catching up with me. Went out to pick up my suit, got changed and went to my Masonic Lodge supper meeting. Made it home quite early, got changed again and relaxed in front of the TV.

Ahhhh, I sat up really late again tonight and promised myself I would try to get to bed early. Goodnight.

30th January 2005 - Sunday


Slept well despite getting to bed quite late indeed.

Had breakfast at a reasonable time and trying my best to get in shape for next weeks chemo battering.

Off to my Mums for a bit and then to a friends house for dinner. Only saw my Mum briefly today, although I would have liked to spend more time, I couldn't bare to see her lying in bed, stiff with Parkinson's and depressed with nothing much to look forward to. At the start of all this, I asked my Brother kindly to spend a little more time with her as he only lives 10 minutes away. His visits have really been only for necessity and few and far between. I asked her if she knew when he would be next visiting and she had no idea. Another stress I could do without, especially when I'm back in to hospital tomorrow for the week.

Had a really nice meal and a chat at my friends house and headed off home to get ready for tomorrow.

Developed a bit of a headache this evening, going to get that much needed sleep now. Night.

31st January 2005 - Monday


Another month almost over! Slept well.

Getting all my stuff together for my week in hospital, I doubt very much they will be ready for me in any way whatsoever. Once I arrive I should have a blood test, X-ray and then start my chemotherapy.

Arrived at the Hospital, bed allocated immediately (6th Floor, West, F4) and bloods taken. Hopefully going for an X-ray soon, then getting ready for first round of chemotherapy (15 hours).

Had my X-ray, I got a look but will not comment until the Consultant has seen it. Now the long wait for chemotherapy drugs to arrive, everyone on my ward is waiting for their chemotherapy to start, it'll be a mad dash when they bring it.

chemotherapy started at 19.20 so all being well it will finish at around 12.00 tomorrow, allowing for bag changes and nursing/staff levels. Cannulation experience was fine (thanks Emla cream and nurse Auxilia) although I'm running out of veins for them to feed me, this time its located around the back of my left forearm.

Consultant has been round to see me and has passed comments on my X-ray. I am now half way through my overall treatment schedule and embarking on the second half this week. My recent X-ray is better but still showing some small evidence of Cancerous lesions, hopefully with the second half of my treatment they will clear and with the figures heading the way they have been there is no reason why they shouldn't (along with a lot of positive thinking on my behalf and all your good wishes I'll beat this Cancer in to non existence). IF it doesn't clear from my lungs, there is a chance that I may have to be operated on, not only on my testicle but possibly my abdomen and my lungs!! A daunting thought indeed.

The testicle operation is expected and for definite, the others based on my recovery. So I'll keep positive and hope that I have enough of you out there keeping everything crossed for me!

Feeling fine tonight, had a few unexpected visitors which was very nice indeed. I'll say goodnight for tonight and be thinking of you all tucked up asleep whilst I am being pumped full of chemo through the night.

As I said this morning, the month is now over, time to embark on another, my time of recovery and freedom from this disease draws closer and closer all the time.

This is my personal experience, not medical advice.
