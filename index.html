Before the diagnosis

Why I've left this here

I first wrote this diary and created this website in 2004, initially using Flash. I later redeveloped it in Apple's iWeb application when it was released, although iWeb has long since become obsolete. The website's purpose was to keep family & friends informed while I was having treatment for Stage IIIb Testicular Cancer - one resource for those who cared to find out how I was feeling and how treatment was progressing - leaving me more time to concentrate on getting better.

For many years, this website has been available at my domain name timstollery.com, but a domain name needs renewing and a website needs maintaining. I can't know whether anyone will continue to look after either of them when I'm no longer able to, and I'm certain the internet will out live me for sure!

This is why I've chosen to preserve and host my story here on GitHub so it is also available at https://timstollery.github.io, in the hope that it might remain accessible even if the domain timstollery.com eventually disappears. I have also archived it where I can.

Over the years, the website itself has evolved considerably too. What started as a fairly simple way of keeping family & friends informed has gradually developed into something much more accessible. I've redesigned it to work across different devices, introduced features to make it easier to read and navigate, and translated the account into a growing number of languages. My hope is that these improvements will allow more people, wherever they are in the world and whatever their individual needs, to access my story and perhaps find something helpful in it.

If this diary website helps someone recognise a symptom, understand what treatment can be like, or feel less alone with a diagnosis, keeping it here will have been worthwhile.

All the very best wishes, Tim Stollery.
