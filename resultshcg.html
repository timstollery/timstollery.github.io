LABORATORY RECORD · DECEMBER 2004 TO FEBRUARY 2025

β-hCG tumour marker results

During my treatment, β-hCG was an important blood marker because the cancer was producing this hormone. The results helped show how my body was responding to chemotherapy. I continued to have blood tests for many years afterwards.

CHEMOTHERAPY EXPLAINED

What B, E and P stand for

BBleomycin

EEtoposide

PCisplatin (a platinum-based drug)

BEP is all three drugs together. EP is etoposide and cisplatin. When my notes refer simply to B chemotherapy, they mean bleomycin given on its own.
