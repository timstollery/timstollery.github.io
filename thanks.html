Thank you

The people who were there

My thanks to the family, friends, fellow patients and hospital staff who were there when I needed them.

A personal thank youAbout 3 min read

Friends at a time like this are a gift greater than the finest things you could ever imagine having, don't ever forget who your friends are.

My thanks to all those that are out there supporting me throughout this rocky journey in my life, your cards, messages, emails and texts have been a great comfort.

My Mum although fighting a debilitating disease herself ( Parkinson's Disease ) has been strong and continues her battle without me to support her, I'll be back Mum don't worry. She's already made it to see me in hospital on numerous occasions! I love you dearly Mum.

My Dad for visiting so soon and all his support.

My extended family although spread far and wide are all out there for me, their kind wishes, thoughts and prayers are with me all the time.

Special thanks to my life long friend Ken Clark who has been on hand and acting in my best interests from the start. There's nothing like having someone to shield you from the shit that can be thrown at you when you're at a low or frightening point in your life.

Milton Mermikides for just being there, unfortunately not in the best circumstances for himself but a shining light of pure positivity.

My Consultant Dr. Savage and his team of Registrars (Dr. Tom Powles & Dr. Gairin Dancey) for my effective treatment & contending with my persistent questions! The Macmillan Nurses, Kara Blackwell and Rachel Sharkey for keeping me sane and more than well informed. My Surgeon Mr. Christmas for his superb handy-work and for extending my warranty! Mr. Christmas's team and 8 North for looking after me post op (including Dr. Rob Jeffs). Karen O'Donnell, amongst the many helpful people I have come across, some of whom I have already thanked. Luckily I met Karen who I came across on 8 North the day before being admitted to hospital for my operation. She is the Senior Sister on the Ward (no wonder). In an ideal world, people like Karen should be running hospitals!

Andy Banks for setting up & Rory Choudhuri for coordinating the guestbook and forum I ran whilst in hospital.

Antoine Rosset of OsiriX fame for assisting me with the rendering of the amazing CT imagery.

Paul & Sue Wood for looking after me so well and always being such great hosts and friends whenever I visit them.

Blood donors, you're doing a fine job. Keep healthy and keep it up. I cannot ever give blood now after my treatment and never really relished the thought of doing it when I was healthy. I can certainly see the benefits now, thank you very much!

My many visitors, some of who have come time and time again … Ken C., Nimish S, Ronnie W., Fini, Michael & Sean T., Olga M., Milton & Bridget M., Leonard W., Martin W. & Hayley, Dean M., Roger R., Nick & Jill J., Martin D., Andy P., Tony C., Brett J., Rob C., Juanita TDE., Simon, Martin S. (brother) & Helen, Emma A., Hayley F., Tori M., Nikki T., Hiren P., Paul W., Khalid R., all at St. Clair Lodge, Arben, Giles (fellow patient) & Marian & John (his parents), Nani Qamar (fellow patient) & all her family/friends for their good wishes.

Others that I cannot mention publicly, or have simply chosen not to mention, I'm sure you know who you are! Thank you.
