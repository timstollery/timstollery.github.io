Scans and results

3D images

Still images and short animations made from my December 2004 CT scan.

X-raysCT scans3D imagesHCG results

A 3D walk through my chest

This 3D virtual walk through my chest gradually reveals the metastases (secondary tumours) in my lungs after passing through my skin, muscles and chest wall. The CT colouring agent tube is initially visible at the top of the movie.

Silent animation. The description above provides its context.

A 3D rotation of my lungs

This short animation is a 3D rotation of my lungs. Using special colouring techniques it enables you to see the extent of the metastases (secondary tumours) that were in my lungs, in much more detail.

Silent animation. The description above provides its context.
